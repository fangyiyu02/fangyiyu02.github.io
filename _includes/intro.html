Biography

I am an M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at New York University. I received my B.S. degree from Lanzhou University.

My research interests center on AI for electronic design automation (EDA), particularly the use of large language models to support hardware design and verification.

I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities starting in Fall 2027.

Research Interests

I am interested in LLM-driven hardware design and verification, from RTL and testbench generation to iterative repair using EDA-tool feedback. A key question is how to control the scope of design changes while preserving design intent.
